# Reloop - E-Waste Logistics Platform Knowledge Base

## 1. About the Company
- **Name:** Reloop
- **Mission:** To provide a seamless, transparent, and eco-friendly platform for electronic waste (e-waste) collection and recycling.
- **Service Areas (Cities):** Currently operating in Delhi and Patna.

## 2. How It Works (The Process)
1. **Schedule Pickup:** The Consumer logs into the platform, adds their address, selects the category of e-waste, and creates a pickup request.
2. **Quotation:** The Segregation Centre in that city reviews the request and provides a quote, which includes a handling charge and a pickup charge.
3. **Acceptance:** The Consumer reviews the quote. If accepted, the request moves to the active pipeline.
4. **Assignment:** The Centre assigns an available Pickup Partner to collect the items. An OTP is generated.
5. **Pickup:** The Partner arrives at the consumer's address, verifies the OTP to ensure security, and collects the items.
6. **Delivery & Completion:** The Partner delivers the e-waste to the Segregation Centre, and the Centre marks the request as "Completed".

## 3. Base Pricing Estimates (Per Kg)
The final material value is calculated automatically based on the estimated weight and the category of the item. Here are the base material prices:
- **Mobile Phone:** ₹150 / kg
- **Laptop:** ₹200 / kg
- **Desktop Computer:** ₹180 / kg
- **LED / LCD TV:** ₹100 / kg
- **Refrigerator:** ₹80 / kg
- **Washing Machine:** ₹80 / kg

- **Printer:** ₹50 / kg

*Note: The actual payout to the consumer is (Material Value - Handling Charge - Pickup Charge).*

## 4. Data Privacy & Security (Important)
- **Data Wiping:** We strongly advise all consumers to perform a factory reset and securely wipe all personal data from devices (Mobile Phones, Laptops, Desktops) before handing them over to our Pickup Partners.
- **OTP Verification:** To prevent unauthorized pickups and ensure security, items will only be collected after the Pickup Partner successfully verifies the OTP provided to the consumer.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
- **Q: How much will it cost to pick up my e-waste?**
  A: The pickup and handling charges are dynamic and quoted by the Segregation Centre based on the size and location of the request. These charges will be deducted from your final material payout.
- **Q: How can I track my request?**
  A: You can track the real-time status of your request (PENDING, QUOTED, ACCEPTED, OUT_FOR_PICKUP, COMPLETED) directly from your Consumer Dashboard.
- **Q: Who do I hand my items to?**
  A: Only hand your items to a verified Reloop Pickup Partner who asks for and verifies your unique OTP.
